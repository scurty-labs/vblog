This is the About page of a demo blog. It exists so the sidebar has something in it and so you can see how a page differs from a post: same editor, same Markdown, but no date, no tags and no place in the feed.

Pages suit the things that do not change often — who you are, how to reach you, what the site is for. This one is a placeholder with a shape you can keep.

## Who writes here

Replace this paragraph with a sentence or two about yourself. A name, what you do when you are not writing, and why this blog exists is enough; a full biography is rarely the reason anyone came.

## What you will find

A blog usually earns a reader's attention with one of two things: a subject they cannot get elsewhere, or a voice they enjoy. Say which one this is. If the posts are notes on a project, say what the project is. If they are essays, say what they are about.
